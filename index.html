    Study

    65/20 mode: if you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20+ years, you only need the 20 questions marked ★. The officer asks 10 and you need 6 correct.

    Motion effects: moving colors, animated text, and confetti. Turn this off for a calmer screen. It also switches off automatically when your device’s Reduce Motion setting is on.

    Voice

    Heart, Bella, Michael, and Fenrir are natural-sounding voices from Kokoro, a free open-source speech model. They’re recorded ahead of time, so nothing you study is sent anywhere. Answers you type yourself (like your senators) are read by your device’s voice.

    How the test works

    The USCIS officer asks up to 20 of the 128 questions out loud. You must answer 12 correctly to pass. The officer stops once you have passed or can no longer pass.

    Reset progress?

    This clears which questions you know and your test history. It can’t be undone.
